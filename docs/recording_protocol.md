# Recording a handstand

1. **Phone on a tripod**, about 3-4 m away, at hip height, portrait orientation.
2. **Side view** for straight handstands: the camera looks at your side, square to your body.
3. **Whole body in frame** for the entire attempt, including the kick-up and the landing, with some space above your feet.
4. **Only you in the frame.** No trainer, spotter or other people in view. The app assumes one person.
5. **Empty-ish room with even light.** Avoid bright windows behind you.
6. **Start recording, then do the attempt, then stop.** One attempt per recording.

For straddle handstands, use a front view instead of a side view.
